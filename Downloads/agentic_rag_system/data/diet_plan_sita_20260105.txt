**Meal Prep Tips:**

1. **Cook in Bulk:** Cook lentils, black beans, and quinoa in bulk to save time and money.
2. **Prep Your Veggies:** Chop and store carrots, celery, cucumbers, and bell peppers in airtight containers.
3. **Make a Smoothie:** Prepare smoothie ingredients like bananas, spinach, and almond milk in advance.
4. **Use a Slow Cooker:** Cook lentil soup, vegetable chili, and quinoa bowls in a slow cooker for hands-off cooking.
5. **Pack Snacks:** Prepare snacks like energy balls, trail mix, and fruit salad in advance to save time.

**Hydration Plan:**

1. **Drink 8-10 Glasses of Water:** Aim to drink at least 8-10 glasses of water throughout the day.
2. **Add Lemon or Lime:** Add a squeeze of lemon or lime juice to your water for extra flavor.
3. **Drink Herbal Tea:** Enjoy herbal teas like peppermint, chamomile, or hibiscus to stay hydrated and calm.
4. **Eat Hydrating Foods:** Include hydrating foods like watermelon, cucumbers, and celery in your meals.

**Snack Ideas:**

1. **Energy Balls:** Mix rolled oats, nuts, and dried fruits to create bite-sized energy balls.
2. **Trail Mix:** Mix nuts, seeds, and dried fruits for a quick and easy snack.
3. **Fruit Salad:** Mix seasonal fruits like strawberries, blueberries, and grapes for a refreshing snack.
4. **Hummus and Veggies:** Dip carrot and celery sticks in hummus for a protein-packed snack.
5. **Yogurt Parfait:** Layer Greek yogurt, granola, and fresh berries for a satisfying snack.
